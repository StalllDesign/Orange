Crowd size

After the walls, furniture and the fenced laser area, about 63 m² of the 108 m² is open floor. At 1 to 1.5 m² per person that is roughly 40 to 60 people at a time. A peak of 300 visitors means the aisles carry the crowd. Ask the organizer for the occupancy limit for this stall size.

Power, weight and the pillar

Laser 8.5 kW plus chiller 9.2 kW is 17.7 kW. On 400 V 3-phase that is about 26 A per phase at best. Ask for a 3-phase point of at least 32 A and have an electrician confirm.

The pillar and distribution point sit inside or beside the north wall. The organizer must keep access to them, so the wall gets a removable panel at the pillar. Confirm this with the organizer.

Machines weigh 1,465 kg and 1,100 kg. Check the hall floor rating and the forklift or crane route.

What I assumed

  • Fabritex is the main brand on every large surface. Orange Apparels appears smaller beneath it on the walls and the counter.
  • North wall: 18.0 × 0.9 m, 4.57 m high, hollow, with three 1.5 × 2.2 m doors on the inside face. No separate store room.
  • West wall: closed, 5.1 m long. It can be left open for a one-wall design.
  • Laser 1,770 wide × 2,126 deep × 3,030 high mm, front facing south. Chiller beside it on the east side.
  • Foam board and wood only. Floor is carpet over the hall floor, no raised deck.
