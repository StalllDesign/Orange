Crowd size

40 staff and 300 visitors at once is 340 people on 108 m², about 0.3 m² each. That is a standing crowd, and the lounge seats only 9. Ask the organizer for the maximum number of people allowed on a stall this size, and decide how many of the 40 staff are on the stand at one time.

Power and weight

Laser 8.5 kW plus chiller 9.2 kW is 17.7 kW. On 400 V 3-phase that is about 26 A per phase at best, more in practice. Ask the organizer for a 3-phase point of at least 32 A at the pillar, and have the fabricator's electrician confirm.

Machines weigh 1,465 kg and 1,100 kg. The mock-up puts no platform under them. Check the hall floor rating and the forklift or crane route from the cargo ramp.
